# Glossary

**Project Brief** — A short public summary of a project: its name, category, the owner's role, its stack, and only verified, shareable facts. Every listed project has one.

**Case Study** — A full engineering write-up of one project. It covers the problem, the owner's contribution, decisions, trade-offs, and verified outcomes. It is published only once its content is verified and the owner has approved it for disclosure. A project with only a Brief has no Case Study page.

**Disclosure restriction** — A limit the owner places on what may be said publicly about a project or employer. The repository counts as public, so a restriction covers source files as well as rendered pages.

**Current employer** — Always shown as "Logistics Company". It is never named anywhere public.

**Creative Piece** — One item of the owner's creative work (an AMV, edit, or motion graphic). It is shown only when the owner supplies it and has the right to display it.

**Placeholder** — A clearly labeled stand-in for an asset the owner has not supplied yet (portrait, résumé, creative media). It is never presented as the owner's work. In production, placeholders appear only where layout needs them, such as the portrait.
